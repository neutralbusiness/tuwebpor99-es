Who is responsible

This policy explains which cookies and similar storage technologies are used by the website www.tuwebpor99.es (TuWebPor99). The controller is Ana Gissela Torres Cruz, whose details appear in the legal notice. For any question about cookies or data protection, write to rgpd@tuwebpor99.es.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser to remember information from one page to the next or between visits. This policy also covers similar technologies, such as the browser's local storage (localStorage), which this website uses for some of its own features.

How Google analytics works here

The website loads Google Tag Manager, a Google service used to manage the Google Analytics 4 tag. Before it loads, the website tells Google that consent is denied by default (Google Consent Mode). Until you press «Accept», Google Analytics neither sets nor reads cookies.

Even if you do not accept, Google may receive cookieless pings (for example, that a page was viewed, with technical browser data and no identifiers), which it uses for aggregated statistical estimates. If you prefer to avoid this entirely, you can block Google services in your browser or with an extension.

Cloudflare statistics

The website also uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which measures visits and loading speed in aggregate without cookies or local storage and without identifying anyone.

Legal basis

Technical, security and preference cookies and storage are used because they are necessary for the website to work and to provide the service you request; they are exempt from consent under article 22.2 of Spanish Law 34/2002 (LSSI).

Google Analytics cookies are only used with your consent (article 22.2 LSSI and article 6.1.a GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

The visit-origin data stored by the website itself (nb_first and nb_last) is only sent to us together with your request when you contact us, and is processed as explained in the privacy policy.

How to accept, reject or withdraw consent

The first time you visit, a notice appears with two buttons of equal prominence: Accept and Reject. If you reject, the website works exactly the same.

After choosing, you will see a small round button with a cookie (🍪) in the bottom-left corner of the screen, «Cookie settings». Press it to see the notice again and change your decision whenever you like: withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it.

You can also delete this website's cookies and local storage from your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). If you delete them, the notice will appear again on your next visit.

If you withdraw consent, Google Analytics stops using cookies from that moment; any already stored disappear when you delete them from your browser.
