Everything you need to know before ordering your website.

What exactly does the €99 + VAT pack include?

Everything you need to be online: custom professional web design, logo with brand style guide and social banners, premium Cloudflare hosting, a .ES domain for one year, business email, SSL certificate, SEO optimisation for search engines and AI with 10 professional articles, and an anti-malware security audit.

Is there any recurring cost?

How long until my website is ready?

Under 3 days from the moment you confirm the project with the 50% payment. That is our delivery commitment.

Of course. If you already have a domain we use it, no problem. If not, we register a custom .ES domain included in the price. For other extensions (.com, .cat, .net…), ask us.

Perfectly. All our websites adapt fully to phones and tablets, with performance above 90 on Google PageSpeed.

Just your business information: what you do, where, photos if you have them and any website that inspires you. We take care of the rest (copy, design, logo, SEO).

Yes. Before starting we close every detail with you by WhatsApp, email or phone, and on delivery we adjust whatever is needed until you are happy.

50% when you confirm the project and the remaining 50% when you receive your website. The yearly maintenance is charged by card through Stripe, a fully secure payment platform.

Do you build websites on any topic?

Yes: local businesses, freelancers, online CVs, personal brands, weddings and celebrations, music bands, sports teams, associations… If you can imagine it, we can design it.

What are the 10 included SEO articles?

Ten professionally written articles targeting the keywords you want to rank for. They are the fuel that makes Google, Bing and AI assistants show your website in the top results.
